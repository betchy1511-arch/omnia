The Science

How Myers Cocktail Works in the Body.

Formulated by Dr. John Myers in the 1960s and refined by Dr. Alan Gaby in the 1990s, the Myers Cocktail combines the micronutrients most commonly deficient in adults with chronic fatigue, migraine or fibromyalgia symptoms.

Magnesium participates in over 300 enzymatic reactions — ATP synthesis, neurotransmitter regulation, muscle relaxation — and functional deficiency is common even with normal serum levels. IV magnesium restores intracellular stores that oral supplementation struggles to reach.

Combined with B-complex, B12 and vitamin C, the formulation replenishes cofactors for energy metabolism, methylation and antioxidant defense in a single 30-minute session.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Myers really the 'original' IV?

+

Yes. Dr. John Myers pioneered it in the 1960s, and it remains the most-studied and most-prescribed wellness infusion in integrative medicine.

How is it different from the Recovery IV?

+

The Myers is a balanced maintenance formulation. Recovery adds higher-volume fluids and optional Rx add-ons for acute symptom relief.

Will I feel the magnesium?

+

A warm, relaxed sensation is common as the magnesium infuses. We adjust the rate so it stays comfortable.
