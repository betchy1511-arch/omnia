Clinical Disclosures

Important Safety Information

Every treatment at Omnia is prescribed and delivered under medical supervision. Please review the information below before your visit and share any concerns with your clinician.

General

All services at Omnia Wellness Center are provided under the medical supervision of our board-certified physician and delivered by licensed clinical staff.

Every client completes a medical intake and — where required — laboratory clearance before any prescription therapy, IV infusion, injectable, laser or hormone service. Treatments are individualized; the information here is educational and does not replace clinical guidance from your provider.

IV Vitamin Therapy

IV therapy is not appropriate for individuals with certain kidney, heart or liver conditions, uncontrolled hypertension, active infection or fluid-restriction disorders. Pregnancy or breastfeeding requires physician clearance.

High-dose vitamin C requires G6PD lab screening prior to first dose. Common, typically mild reactions include bruising at the IV site, cool-flush sensation, mineral taste, transient headache, dizziness or lightheadedness. Rare risks include vein irritation, allergic reaction and infiltration. Notify your clinician immediately if you experience chest tightness, difficulty breathing, rash, swelling or severe pain during your infusion.

Peptide Therapy (BPC-157, TB-500, MOTs-C and related)

Peptides are prescribed on an individualized basis after medical consultation and are compounded and dispensed through a licensed partner pharmacy. They are not FDA-approved for the indications for which they are commonly prescribed.

Do not use if pregnant, breastfeeding, actively being treated for cancer, or with a personal history of hormone-sensitive cancer without explicit physician approval. Common side effects may include injection-site redness, mild GI upset, transient fatigue or headache. Discontinue and contact us if you experience rash, swelling, shortness of breath or persistent GI symptoms.

GLP-1 Weight Management (Semaglutide / Tirzepatide)

GLP-1 therapy is prescribed for eligible adults after medical clearance. It is contraindicated in personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma or Multiple Endocrine Neoplasia syndrome type 2 (MEN 2), and in pregnancy or breastfeeding.

Common side effects include nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, constipation, fatigue and appetite suppression, typically improving as your body adjusts. Serious but rare risks include pancreatitis, gallbladder disease, kidney injury from dehydration and severe hypoglycemia when combined with insulin or sulfonylureas. Seek immediate care for severe abdominal pain, persistent vomiting, jaundice or signs of allergic reaction.

Cosmetic Injectables (Neuromodulators & Dermal Fillers)

Injectable treatments are not for individuals with active skin infection at the injection site, known hypersensitivity to the product or its components, certain neuromuscular disorders, active autoimmune flares, pregnancy or breastfeeding.

Common effects include temporary redness, swelling, bruising, tenderness and mild asymmetry. Rare risks include infection, nodule formation, vascular occlusion, scarring, headache, ptosis (eyelid or brow droop) and, with dermal fillers, rare vision or vascular complications. Seek immediate care for severe pain, skin color change, vision changes or signs of infection.

Laser & Fotona Treatments

Laser treatments require photoprotection before and after each session. Recent sun exposure, self-tanner, active infection, isotretinoin use within six months, photosensitizing medications and certain skin conditions may disqualify or postpone treatment.

Common effects include redness, warmth, mild swelling and transient sensitivity. Less common risks include blistering, pigmentary change (lightening or darkening), scarring and, rarely, infection. Strict SPF 50+ use is required in the treated area during recovery.

Hormone Optimization (Men & Women)

Hormone therapy is initiated only after comprehensive laboratory evaluation and medical clearance. It is contraindicated in personal history of hormone-sensitive cancer, uncontrolled cardiovascular disease, active thromboembolic disease, pregnancy or breastfeeding.

Ongoing lab monitoring is required. Potential effects vary by protocol and may include changes in mood, sleep, libido, blood pressure, hematocrit, lipids or fertility. Report any chest pain, shortness of breath, leg swelling, severe headache or vision changes immediately.

Consent, Emergencies & Contact

Informed consent is obtained in writing before any procedure. You may pause or discontinue any treatment at any time.

For a medical emergency call 911 immediately. For non-urgent clinical concerns following a treatment at Omnia, contact us at (818) 386-8511 or via the client portal.

This page is provided for educational purposes only and does not constitute medical advice or establish a provider-patient relationship. Individual results vary. Always consult with your Omnia clinician regarding your specific condition and treatment plan.
