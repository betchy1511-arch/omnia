Fotona Laser & Skin Rejuvenation

Fotona SmoothEye

The eyes are often the first place to show fatigue, fine lines, and crepey texture. Fotona SmoothEye is a specialized non-ablative laser treatment developed for the delicate skin around the eyes, working to smooth, tighten, and refresh, without needles, surgery, or downtime.

The Omnia Approach

What Is SmoothEye?

In Detail

SmoothEye uses precisely controlled Er:YAG energy safely calibrated for the periorbital area to warm the dermis and stimulate new collagen. As the tissue firms, fine lines soften, crepey texture smooths, and mild upper and lower lid laxity improves. The treatment is performed with protective eye shields for complete safety, and because there’s no ablation, most clients return to their day immediately.

Frequently Asked Questions

What Does Fotona SmoothEye Treat?

+

Fotona SmoothEye is designed for gentle, needle-free tightening for the delicate eye area. It’s a great fit for clients looking for visible improvement without needles, surgery, or long downtime.

Is It Comfortable?

+

Yes. Fotona SmoothEye is performed with cooling, careful pacing, and comfort as a priority. Most clients describe the sensation as warm, tingling, or a series of quick pulses.

How Many Sessions Will I Need?

+

It depends on your concern and your goals. Most clients see visible improvement after a series of sessions, and we’ll map out a personalized plan at your consultation.

Is There Downtime?

+

Downtime is minimal for most Fotona treatments — typically some warmth, mild redness, or light flaking depending on protocol depth. Your provider will walk you through exactly what to expect.

How Is This Different from Other Lasers?

+

Fotona’s dual-wavelength platform (Er:YAG and Nd:YAG) allows us to layer multiple treatment depths and modes in a single visit — which is why the results tend to look more balanced and natural than single-wavelength systems.
