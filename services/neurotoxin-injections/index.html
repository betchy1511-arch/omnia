What Are Neurotoxin Injections & Their Benefits?

In Detail

Neurotoxin injections, also called wrinkle relaxer injections or botulinum toxin injections, are a cosmetic neurotoxin treatment that temporarily relaxes the facial muscles responsible for expression lines. As the muscle softens, the skin above it smooths out, reducing the appearance of wrinkles. This quick, non-surgical facial wrinkle treatment uses trusted neuromodulators to deliver natural-looking results with no downtime. The benefits of these muscle relaxing injections go beyond simply smoothing wrinkles. Clients choose our anti-wrinkle injections for frown line treatment and glabellar line injections between the brows, forehead line injections, and crow’s feet treatment around the eyes. Many also opt for preventative neurotoxin treatments or baby tox to soften fine lines before they deepen, while others explore a brow lift, lip flip treatment, or masseter Neurotoxin for facial slimming and TMJ relief. Curious which treatment is right for you? Schedule a free consultation with our expert injectors.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What Are Neurotoxin Injections Used For?

+

Neurotoxin injections are used to soften and prevent wrinkles by relaxing the facial muscles that cause them. Common uses include frown line treatment, forehead line injections, and crow’s feet treatment, along with specialized options like masseter Neurotoxin and a lip flip.

Which Neurotoxin Is Right for Me?

+

All of our neurotoxins are FDA-approved botulinum toxin injections that relax muscles to smooth wrinkles. They differ slightly in formulation, dosing, onset, and longevity. During your consultation, we’ll recommend the neuromodulator best suited to your goals.

Do Neurotoxin Injections Hurt?

+

Most clients find the treatment very tolerable. The needles are tiny and the injections are quick, feeling like a small pinch. We can apply a topical numbing cream or use ice for added comfort.

How Long Do Neurotoxin Injections Last?

+

Results from anti-wrinkle injections typically last three to four months. With regular treatments, many clients find their results last longer over time as the treated muscles become accustomed to relaxing.

What Is Preventative or Baby Neurotoxin?

+

Preventative Neurotoxin, often called baby Neurotoxin, uses smaller doses to soften fine lines early and slow the formation of deeper wrinkles. It’s a popular choice for younger clients who want to stay ahead of visible aging with a subtle, natural look.
