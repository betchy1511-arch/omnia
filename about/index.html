About Us

Where Wellness Meets Beauty in Encino

Omnia Wellness Center was founded on a simple belief: that confidence begins with feeling your best. Located in the heart of Encino, our luxury med spa blends advanced aesthetic treatments, regenerative medicine, IV therapy, and wellness services in a sophisticated yet welcoming environment — designed to help every client look refreshed, feel empowered, and age gracefully.

Guided by experienced, board-certified providers, we deliver personalized care that supports wellness from the inside out.

Our Vision

Built By Clinicians Who Wanted Something Better.

Omnia was created by two healthcare professionals who share a common vision: a place where advanced medical aesthetics and whole-person wellness live side by side, delivered with the highest standards of safety, innovation, and a truly exceptional client experience.

At Omnia Wellness Center, every treatment is guided by a commitment to safety, education, natural-looking results, and the long-term health and vitality of our clients.

Our Story

Founded On A Shared Vision

Omnia Wellness Center was founded by Yaroslava Markov, MSN, APRN, FNP-BC, whose decade of clinical healthcare experience and commitment to exceptional patient care inspired the practice. She was joined by Yazmin Alexandra Islas, MSN, RN, Co-Founder, who brings more than 26 years of healthcare experience and a passion for medical aesthetics, wellness, and personalized care.

Together, they created Omnia with a shared vision of combining advanced aesthetics, regenerative medicine, and wellness in one luxury destination. Their mission is to provide safe, evidence-based treatments with the highest level of clinical excellence, helping every client look refreshed, feel confident, and age beautifully.

Our Philosophy
